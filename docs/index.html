Abstract

Creating visual effects for 3D content requires specialized design knowledge and technical expertise. SpeLL produces editable 3D VFX drafts from text prompts using large language models and retrieval-based asset assembly. It combines prompt enhancement, spatial design for component retrieval and layout arrangement, and temporal design for activation sequencing.

Informed by a formative study with professional VFX designers, SpeLL generates parameterized meshes, textures, and particle systems that can be refined in Unity. A user study with twenty professional designers evaluated the framework against ablated systems, 3D generation baselines, and manual workflows. Results showed higher intention alignment, spatial-temporal layout quality, and editability. The goal is to support early exploration and drafting while leaving final expressive control to designers.
